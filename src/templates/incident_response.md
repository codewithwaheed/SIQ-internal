# {{policy_title}}

**Introduction**  
This Incident Response Policy establishes procedures for detecting, analyzing, containing, and recovering from cybersecurity incidents affecting {{business_name}} systems and data.

**Purpose**  
To ensure rapid, coordinated, and effective response to security incidents, minimize business impact, preserve evidence, and facilitate recovery operations.

**Scope**  
This policy applies to all security incidents affecting {{business_name}} information systems, networks, applications, and data, regardless of location or ownership.

**Definitions**  
- **Security Incident**: Any event that threatens the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information assets
- **Incident Response Team (IRT)**: Designated personnel responsible for incident response activities
- **Business Continuity**: Maintaining essential business operations during and after an incident

**Policy Statement**  
{{business_name}} maintains a structured incident response capability to:

1. **Detection and Analysis**
   - Continuous monitoring for security events
   - Incident classification and prioritization
   - Initial impact assessment within {{initial_response_time}} minutes
   - Evidence collection and preservation

2. **Containment and Eradication**
   - Immediate containment of security threats
   - System isolation procedures when necessary
   - Malware removal and vulnerability patching
   - Coordination with law enforcement when appropriate

3. **Recovery and Lessons Learned**
   - Gradual restoration of affected systems
   - Monitoring for recurring incidents
   - Post-incident review and documentation
   - Process improvement recommendations

**Procedures**  
1. **Incident Detection**
   - Monitor security alerts and logs continuously
   - Investigate suspicious activities promptly
   - Validate and classify potential incidents
   - Escalate confirmed incidents to IRT

2. **Incident Classification**
   - **Low**: Minimal business impact, no data compromise
   - **Medium**: Limited impact, potential data exposure
   - **High**: Significant impact, confirmed data breach
   - **Critical**: Severe impact, major system compromise

3. **Response Timeline**
   - Initial response: {{initial_response_time}} minutes
   - Containment: {{containment_time}} hours
   - Recovery planning: {{recovery_planning_time}} hours
   - Full recovery: {{full_recovery_time}} days

**Responsibilities**  
- **CISO/Security Manager**: Overall incident response program oversight
- **Incident Response Team**: Execute response procedures, coordinate activities
- **IT Operations**: System monitoring, technical response, recovery operations
- **Legal/Compliance**: Regulatory notification, legal implications assessment
- **Communications**: Internal and external stakeholder notifications
- **All Employees**: Report suspected incidents, follow containment procedures

**Consequences of Non-Compliance**  
Failure to follow incident response procedures may result in:
- Extended incident duration and increased damage
- Regulatory penalties and legal liability
- Loss of customer trust and business reputation
- Disciplinary action for willful non-compliance
