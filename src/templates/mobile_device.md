# {{policy_title}}

**Introduction**  
This Mobile Device Management Policy governs the use of mobile devices to access {{business_name}} systems, applications, and data, ensuring security while enabling productivity.

**Purpose**  
To establish security requirements for mobile devices, protect organizational data, enable secure remote work capabilities, and maintain compliance with regulatory requirements.

**Scope**  
This policy applies to all mobile devices including smartphones, tablets, and laptops used to access {{business_name}} resources, whether company-owned or personally-owned (BYOD).

**Definitions**

- **Mobile Device**: Portable computing devices including smartphones, tablets, laptops, and wearables
- **Mobile Device Management (MDM)**: Software solution for managing and securing mobile devices
- **BYOD**: Bring Your Own Device - personal devices used for business purposes
- **Remote Wipe**: Ability to remotely delete data from a lost or stolen device

**Policy Statement**  
All mobile devices accessing {{business_name}} resources must comply with security requirements:

1. **Device Management**
   - Approved device types: {{device_types_allowed}}
   - MDM enrollment required for all business devices
   - Regular security updates and patches mandatory
   - Jailbreaking/rooting strictly prohibited

2. **Security Controls**
   - Device encryption required: {{encryption_required}}
   - Screen lock with PIN/password/biometric authentication
   - Automatic lock timeout: maximum {{screen_lock_timeout}} minutes
   - Remote wipe capability: {{remote_wipe_enabled}}

3. **Application Management**
   - App installation policy: {{app_store_policy}}
   - Regular app updates required
   - Prohibition of unauthorized app stores
   - Business and personal data separation

**Procedures**

1. **Device Enrollment**
   - Complete MDM enrollment before accessing business resources
   - Install required security certificates and profiles
   - Configure email, VPN, and business applications
   - Accept device monitoring and management terms

2. **Ongoing Compliance**
   - Maintain current operating system versions
   - Install security patches within {{patch_window}} days
   - Report lost or stolen devices within {{device_loss_reporting}} hours
   - Comply with periodic security scans

3. **BYOD Requirements**
   - Separate business and personal data containers
   - Allow remote management of business data only
   - Maintain device in good working condition
   - Accept potential data loss during security incidents

**Responsibilities**

- **IT Security Team**: Configure MDM policies, monitor compliance, respond to incidents
- **IT Support**: Provide device setup assistance, troubleshoot technical issues
- **Employees**: Follow security requirements, report incidents, maintain device security
- **Managers**: Ensure team compliance, approve BYOD requests, support policy enforcement

**Consequences of Non-Compliance**  
Policy violations may result in:

- Immediate suspension of mobile device access
- Remote wipe of business data and applications
- Disciplinary action including termination
- Personal liability for security breaches
- Loss of BYOD privileges
